# Wearabouts Product Hunt video plan

## Goal

Create a 20-second landscape launch video that makes the product understandable without narration. The video should feel like Wearabouts: warm, calm, fashion-led and practical rather than like a generic AI demo.

## Storyboard

| Time | Beat | Visual evidence | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:04 | Problem | Existing Wearabouts landing-page lifestyle image | A full wardrobe. Still nothing to wear? |
| 0:04-0:08 | Wardrobe first | Existing wardrobe catalogue image | Start with what you already own. |
| 0:08-0:12.5 | Plan an outfit | Wardrobe image, an occasion prompt and Singapore forecast context | Outfits from your wardrobe. Shaped by the forecast. |
| 0:12.5-0:16.8 | Check a purchase | Existing “Should I buy it?” product screen | Check before you buy. |
| 0:16.8-0:20 | Product close | Wearabouts logo and brand palette | Make more outfits from the clothes you own. |

## Claims boundary

- Use only existing product screens and brand assets.
- Do not invent user counts, performance statistics, testimonials or retailer coverage.
- Do not imply virtual try-on, exact fit prediction or automatic purchasing.
- Keep AI implied through the real workflows rather than making a broad “AI-powered” claim.
